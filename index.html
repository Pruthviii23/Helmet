What's inside

Two tiny brains. One handshake.

One box lives in the helmet and checks that you're wearing it. The other lives on the bike and holds the power.

Pings stop? Bike locks.

The bike doesn't wait for a "helmet off" message. It just listens for the steady ping. If the helmet goes quiet for about a second and a half, the relay opens and the self-start goes dead.

Try to fool it.

Every shortcut people try, and why it doesn't work.

Buckle it and leave it on the seat

Stays locked

The strap is shut, but nobody's head is pressing the pad.

Press the pad with your hand

Stays locked

The pad feels pressure, but the strap is still open.

Let the helmet battery die

Stays locked

No battery means no ping. No ping means no start.

Use a friend's Helmate

Stays locked

Each bike is paired with one helmet and ignores the rest.

For the builders

Wiring, pins and the logic. Open it if you're holding a soldering iron.

Helmet board

Pressure padOne leg to 3V3, other leg to GPIO3. Add a 10 kΩ from GPIO3 to GND. Reading goes up when pressed.
Hall sensorVCC to 3V3, GND to GND, OUT to GPIO4. Most modules read LOW when the magnet is close.
MagnetGlue it to the other strap end so it sits on the sensor when buckled.
PowerSmall Li-ion cell into the SuperMini's 5V/battery input.

Bike board

Relay INGPIO5. Relay VCC to 5V, GND to GND.
Relay contactsPut COM and NO in the self-start wire. Normally open means locked by default.
PowerBike 12 V through a 12 V → 5 V converter into the 5V pin.
PairingThe bike only accepts messages from its own helmet's MAC address.
// helmet: every 500 ms
bool headIn  = analogRead(3) > PAD_THRESHOLD;
bool clicked = digitalRead(4) == LOW;
if (headIn && clicked) esp_now_send(bikeMac, "WORN");

// bike: on every message from helmetMac
lastPing = millis();

// bike: main loop
bool unlocked = millis() - lastPing < 1500;
digitalWrite(5, unlocked ? HIGH : LOW);   // relay closed only while pings arrive

Pin numbers are suggestions. Avoid GPIO 2, 8 and 9 on the SuperMini, since they affect boot. Tune PAD_THRESHOLD by printing the pad reading with and without the helmet on.
